# ADR 0021: OCR second-pass non-borrows

## Status

**Accepted (2026-10-23).** Successor decision record for the non-borrows of the second OCR-borrow series. It records what the series deliberately did not take from OCR; it adds no implementation, no configuration, and no benchmark round.

## Context

The second OCR-borrow series examined seven candidate borrowings (S1–S7) across feedback, calibration, attention and recovery, and recorded its decisions in ADRs 0014–0020. The series retires its temporary tracker now that every item has a verdict: every item is ☑ or ✗, each executed item's ADR is merged, and the tracker's deletion checklist requires its non-borrow list to be absorbed into a successor decision document before the file is removed.

The first OCR-borrow series retired the same way. PR [#128](https://github.com/beiyanpiki/pir/pull/128) deleted that tracker and absorbed its non-borrow principle into the surviving ADRs; [commit 340b1bc](https://github.com/beiyanpiki/pir/commit/340b1bc) shows the pattern, pinning the surveyed OCR revision and stating the principle directly instead of pointing at a deleted list. This ADR follows that precedent: each direction below keeps the reason it was refused, the decision that governs it, and the evidence that would reopen it, so the direction can be re-litigated on new evidence rather than on the absence of a record.

A non-borrow is a direction, not an implementation: rejecting one says the direction is not justified on today's measured evidence, not that it can never be right. The revisit column names the measurement or consumer that would make the question live again. Where a direction already has a decision ADR, that ADR's conditions govern the reopen; this record does not amend them.

## Decision

The ten directions below are deliberately not borrowed. Each row carries (a) the direction, (b) the reason recorded when the series declined it, (c) the governing ADR or product boundary where one exists, and (d) the condition that would reopen it.

| Direction | Reason not borrowed | Governing ADR / boundary | Revisit condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reflection filter as a cost funnel | M0's offline material shows a small removal surface (8.0% kill / 3.0% false kill). Those are offline percentages, not online false-kill rates, and they do not establish verifier cost savings per candidate; a pre-filter would also add a new loss channel. | No separate ADR: the data is [ADR 0007](0007-aacr-verifier-offline-benchmark.md)'s offline verifier benchmark, and the missing evidence is the cost comparison the direction needs. | Measured per-candidate costs showing the filter is much cheaper than the verifier and online false-kill behaviour is acceptable while the removal surface stays small. |
| Semantic grouping / large-PR slicing | ADR 0011's data trigger did not fire: M1's weak recall is not large-PR-specific, and grouping would add cross-group drift risk on top of an unmeasured benefit. | [ADR 0011](0011-large-changeset-slicing-decision.md) (D-B6, rejected). | ADR 0011's own conditions: a material containing ≥2,000-line PRs, density (findings per 1,000 changed lines and per PR) measured as materially worse, and budget stops in a material share of that subset under the default ceiling; reopen only when density and budget both hold. |
| Cross-file relocation | [ADR 0005](0005-snippet-anchored-location-correction.md) §4: a unique-hit relocation can retarget a finding to a file the model never analysed, and the evidence gate is the better constraint. | ADR 0005 §4. | A measured anchor-error class in which findings are uniquely located in another changed file; ADR 0005 is amended only with measurement evidence, anchor metrics included. |
| In-session three-zone compression | pir opens a fresh session per round, so the long-conversation bloat that OCR's compression exists to manage does not exist structurally. | [ADR 0006](0006-default-token-budget-with-grace.md) Context (the budget decision records the refusal while adopting controlled truncation). | pir reuses one long conversation across rounds or sessions, with a measured token cost that the fresh-session model cannot avoid; then compression becomes a new context-management decision with its own evidence. |
| Coverage manifest / per-file terminal states | The existing coverage ledger and exclusion record already derive the terminal states; [ADR 0013](0013-change-coverage-manifest-decision.md) rejects a second state machine as two sources of truth. The audit-resume discovery is execution recovery, not this contract. | ADR 0013 (D-B8, rejected as a new contract). | ADR 0013's conditions: a real consumer that cannot join the two existing artifacts, a shared cross-mode schema, or an exclusion case that the `excluded`/`excludedReason` pair cannot express. |
| Session viewer / audit enhancements | [ADR 0012](0012-session-audit-enhancements-decision.md): no recorded demand, and transcripts are retained SDK snapshots, not provider wire captures. | ADR 0012 (D-B7, rejected). | ADR 0012's conditions: a recorded debugging incident where the manifest, findings, `verify-fix` and stderr tails cannot locate the cause and replay demonstrably would have; a prompt-tuning campaign that needs cross-session replay; external consumer demand; and if transcripts become the default, a retention/size budget plus a delete/export story in the same change. |
| Delegation mode / ACP / multi-provider / ChatGPT auth | Different product form factors; pir's skill + JSON envelope + serve already cover the equivalent. | Product boundary; no review-quality ADR. | A named consumer needs a product form that the skill + JSON envelope + serve lane cannot serve; that is a product-form decision with its own ADR, not a review-quality borrowing. |
| Auto-fix / `suggestion_code` | pir's reviewer prompt deliberately says "Do not propose fixes"; adding suggestions changes the product boundary and would need its own verification. | Product boundary; no review-quality ADR. | A product decision to change the reviewer's no-fix boundary, with its own verification design for suggestion quality; review-quality evidence alone does not reopen it. |
| LLM grouping call | Same as semantic grouping: the metadata-only trick is clever, but the need is unproven. | [ADR 0011](0011-large-changeset-slicing-decision.md). | The same measurement conditions as ADR 0011, plus measured evidence that a metadata-only grouping call beats the no-grouping control without introducing cross-group drift. |
| Re-location LLM fallback | B2 chose reject-and-resubmit over letting the model guess a snippet again; OCR's own prompt demands VERBATIM, showing how error-prone the fallback is. | [ADR 0005](0005-snippet-anchored-location-correction.md) (B2). | A measured anchor-error class that reject-and-resubmit cannot clear, with anchor metrics showing the fallback is accurate enough to be worth the added loss channel; ADR 0005 is amended only with measurement evidence. |

## Consequences

- The tracker's precondition that its non-borrows be absorbed is met: the ten directions survive here instead of in a temporary file, and the file can be deleted with its decisions intact.
- These directions are closed at their current evidence level. A proposal that cites one must meet its revisit condition; citing parity with OCR is not new evidence, and re-litigating without the named measurement is out of scope.
- No code, configuration, command-line contract or benchmark population changes. The governing decisions (ADR 0005 §4, ADR 0011, ADR 0012, ADR 0013 and ADR 0006) stand exactly as accepted.
- The numbers quoted above keep their benchmark caliber: M0 is offline verifier material ([ADR 0007](0007-aacr-verifier-offline-benchmark.md)) and M1 is the locked 50-PR end-to-end protocol ([ADR 0009](0009-m1-end-to-end-benchmark.md)). Neither establishes online behaviour, and no number here is a production claim.
